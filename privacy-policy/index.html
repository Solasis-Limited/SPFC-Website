Sex Positions for Couples

Privacy Policy

Sex Positions for Couples is operated by Solasis Limited. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app and related services collect, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Scope

This policy applies to the Sex Positions for Couples mobile app and web app, related backend services, and support and account-deletion requests submitted through our website.

Information We Collect

  • Account information, including your email address and account identifier. You may also provide a display name; sign-in providers may supply a name for your profile.
  • Profile and preference information, such as comfort and experience levels, goals, adult-confirmation and consent choices.
  • App activity and intimate preferences, including positions you save, want to try, mark as tried, or rate; partner links, sharing settings, and couple votes.
  • Assistant questions, recent chat context, and relevant preference information when you use the assistant.
  • Analytics consent choices and product usage events, such as screen views and feature interactions, when product analytics are enabled.
  • If you enable notifications: device or browser identifiers, push tokens or browser push subscriptions, platform, notification choices, time zone, and limited activity and reminder timestamps. When recommendation reminders are enabled, a server-side position target is also used to prepare a reminder.
  • If you subscribe: account and billing-provider identifiers, subscription or transaction references, product and entitlement status, and subscription dates. Payment details are handled by the applicable app store or billing provider; we do not receive your full payment-card number through the app.
  • If you contact support through our website: the name and email address you provide, request type, subject, message, account email if supplied, and submission time and page URL.

How We Use Information

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Sync your saved preferences across devices.
  • Support couple linking and explicit sharing permissions.
  • Personalize position recommendations.
  • Provide educational assistant responses.
  • Maintain app security and detect errors.
  • Improve product quality when analytics consent is enabled.
  • Deliver notifications and manage subscriptions when you choose those features.
  • Respond to support, privacy, and account-deletion requests.

Service providers and sharing

We use service providers to operate features you choose. Supabase provides account authentication, database, and backend services. RevenueCat and the applicable app store or web billing provider process subscription information. When enabled and configured, product analytics may be sent to a PostHog-compatible analytics service, and assistant prompts may be processed by the AI provider configured for the service. Expo and the relevant platform push services (Apple Push Notification service or Firebase Cloud Messaging) help deliver notifications. EmailJS delivers support requests submitted through our website. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

The website also loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those fonts, Google may receive technical connection information such as your IP address.

Sharing and Partner Data

Partner-linked data is private by default and only shared according to the permissions you explicitly enable.

We may share selected preferences between linked partners only when the relevant sharing controls are turned on. You can update or remove partner linking from the app.

We do not share intimate preferences with a partner unless you enable the relevant sharing controls.

Analytics

The app includes a consent-based analytics system. Product analytics are off by default and are only sent when you enable analytics consent in onboarding or settings.

A limited set of account access events, such as sign-in request, sign-in completion, sign-in failure, and sign-out events, may be processed before analytics consent so that we can operate, secure, and improve the reliability of account access. These events may include the sign-in provider, email domain, or error information.

When analytics is enabled, events may be associated with your account identifier. Assistant question text is included in an assistant-usage event when you submit it and may therefore be sent to the configured analytics service if analytics is enabled. Analytics configuration may vary by app release. You can change your analytics choice in the app settings.

AI and Assistant Features

The assistant is educational and non-explicit. Assistant prompts may be processed by our backend and, when an AI provider is configured and used, sent with recent chat context and relevant preference information to that provider to generate guidance and recommendations. Otherwise, the app may use built-in guidance without an external AI provider.

Please avoid including information in a prompt that you do not want processed to answer your question. AI providers may handle submitted information under their own privacy terms.

Notifications and subscriptions

Notifications are optional. If you enable them, we store the registration and settings needed to deliver them to your devices. Notifications are designed to avoid showing position names, vote values, invite codes, or other intimate details on the lock screen. You can turn off categories in the app or revoke notification permission in your device or browser settings. Signing out deactivates the current device registration. Recommendation reminders are optional and may be sent after at least three days without app activity, at most once every seven days, and outside the default quiet hours of 9 pm to 8 am in your device time zone. The reminder target is kept server-side and is resolved by the app only after you open the notification.

If you subscribe, the applicable app store or billing provider processes the purchase. We use subscription status and related provider identifiers to provide and manage access. Subscription cancellation and payment records are subject to the provider where you purchased.

Data Retention

We retain account and app data for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

You can start account deletion in the app where available, or use the support and data-deletion form on our website. We delete or anonymize account-associated information where appropriate and subject to legal, security, and service-provider retention requirements. Copies held by third-party providers may be subject to their retention policies.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect data, including authenticated access controls and server-side processing for sensitive features.

Your Choices

  • Sign out.
  • Disable analytics consent.
  • Unlink your partner.
  • Update sharing permissions.
  • Request account deletion.
  • Enable or disable notification categories and revoke device or browser notification permission.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription with the provider where you purchased it.

Children

Sex Positions for Couples is intended only for adults of legal age in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, use the support and data deletion form on this site.
